The founder directs and approves; the AI team drafts. Recreate these roles in any new session by giving each one this file plus `DECISIONS.md`.

Outside the AI team: a paid native-speaker proofreader and voice talent (Fiverr), community spot checks (r/Korean, HelloTalk, Tandem), and Gemini as a second-opinion reviewer.

## Joy's fun checklist (every mechanic, after passing Lina and Tess)
- Instant feedback: a sound and visual for every input; native audio on every success.
- It matters to someone: each spell visibly changes a character's day or the town.
- Safe failure: a charming fizzle and a one-line hint.
- A personal touch: choices in order, decoration, wording or relationships.
- Cozy sessions: 5–15 minutes, a natural stopping point, something new roughly every 10 minutes.
